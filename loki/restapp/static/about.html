About Us

Ambur Star Biryani traces its origin back to the royal kitchens of the Arcot Nawabs in the late 19th century. Passed down through generations, this culinary art flourished in the small town of Ambur, Tamil Nadu. What makes our biryani unique is its delicate balance of spices, the exclusive use of short-grain Seeraga Samba rice, and the traditional dum cooking method.

Founded by Hussain Baig, Ambur Star Biryani became a landmark eatery on the Chennai-Bengaluru highway. His insistence on high-quality ingredients, freshly ground spices, and marinated meat earned him a loyal following. The biryani was initially prepared for soldiers and travelers — simple, aromatic, and always soul-satisfying.

Today, Ambur Star Biryani is a trusted brand across India, maintaining the same age-old recipe with utmost authenticity. Whether you're dining in or taking away, our biryani is served with passion, tradition, and a taste that lingers.
